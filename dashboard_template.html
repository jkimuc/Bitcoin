03 / Method

A simple model, fully specified

Ordinary least squares fits every available positive daily PriceUSD observation with equal weight.

t = calendar days since 2010-07-18 / 365.25
ln(Pₜ) = α + βt + εₜ
2030 point estimate = exp(α + βt₂₀₃₀)

The date convention is November 1, 2030, rather than an average for the month.

The exponentiated fitted log value is a conditional median under a symmetric zero-median log-error model. It is not the arithmetic expected dollar price. No lognormal mean correction is applied.

Confidence and prediction formulas

With x = [1, t], the classical 95% mean-log interval is xβ ± tn−2, .975 √(x CovIID(β) x′). CovIID(β) = s²(X′X)⁻¹ and s² = SSE/(n−2).

The HAC interval uses xβ ± 1.959964 √(x CovNW(β) x′), with Newey–West covariance, Bartlett weights over 90 daily lags and n/(n−2) correction. The lag choice is a disclosed convention, not an optimized guarantee.

The IID prediction interval adds s² inside the variance term. All endpoints are exponentiated. The mean-trend band describes parameter uncertainty in the fitted log trend; the prediction interval adds a stationary IID log-error assumption for a single future observation. No HAC future-price prediction interval is claimed. Bands are pointwise, not simultaneous.

04 / Interpretation

Why the uncertainty is larger

  • Daily observations are dependent. Treating thousands of daily prices as independent observations makes classical bands misleadingly narrow.
  • The growth rate changes. This model assumes constant percentage growth over time. Early price discovery can dominate a full-history fit. Changing the sample exposes that sensitivity.
  • Extrapolation is structural risk. November 2030 lies about 4.4 years beyond the last observed date. Neither covariance estimator includes future regulatory, technical, or market regime changes.
  • HAC helps only part of the problem. It adjusts coefficient uncertainty for heteroskedasticity and serial correlation within the stated lag scheme; it does not establish that a nonstationary price trend will persist.
  • Early data is less robust. Bitcoin’s first markets were thin and fragmented. A provider’s daily reference price is not an executable market-wide closing price. The available series starts in 2010, not at the 2009 genesis block.

The dollar estimate is exp(fitted natural-log price), not 10 raised to that value and not the log value itself. It is a geometric-mean trend; calling it a median requires the stated zero-median log-error assumption. A lognormal arithmetic mean would additionally require exp(μ + σ²/2), but that unvalidated distributional assumption is not used for the dashboard’s point estimate.

The audit independently reads the raw CSV using Python’s CSV module and recomputes with statsmodels rather than importing this project’s fit functions. It checks all three fitting windows, classical covariance, Newey–West HAC covariance, both confidence methods, IID prediction endpoints, every full-history daily export and the holdout results. See the calculation_verification.json download for exact values, tolerances and differences. Correct arithmetic does not make the model reliable.
